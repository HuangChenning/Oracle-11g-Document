`USER_MVIEW_DETAIL_RELATIONS` represents the named detail relations that are either in the `FROM` list of a materialized view, or that are indirectly referenced through views in the `FROM` list. Its columns are the same as those in ["ALL\_MVIEW\_DETAIL\_RELATIONS"](statviews_1147.md).
