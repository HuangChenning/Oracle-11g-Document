`USER_USERS` describes the current user. Its columns (except for `PASSWORD`, `PROFILE`, `PASSWORD_VERSIONS`, `EDITIONS_ENABLED`, `AUTHENTICATION_TYPE`, and `EDITIONS_ENABLED`) are the same as those in `DBA_USERS`.
