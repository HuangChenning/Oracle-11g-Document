`DBA_IND_SUBPARTITIONS` describes, for each index subpartition in the database, the partition-level partitioning information, the storage parameters for the subpartition, and various partition statistics generated by the `DBMS_STATS` package. Its columns are the same as those in ["ALL\_IND\_SUBPARTITIONS"](statviews_1108.md).
