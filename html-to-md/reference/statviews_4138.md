`DBA_MVIEW_AGGREGATES` describes the grouping functions (aggregated measures) that appear in the `SELECT` list of all aggregated materialized view in the database. Its columns are the same as those in ["ALL\_MVIEW\_AGGREGATES"](statviews_1143.md).
