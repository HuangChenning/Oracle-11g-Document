`USER_ADVISOR_EXEC_PARAMETERS` displays the parameter values used for past executions of tasks owned by the current user. Its columns (except for `OWNER`) are the same as those in ["DBA\_ADVISOR\_EXEC\_PARAMETERS"](statviews_3016.md).
