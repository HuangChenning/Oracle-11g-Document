`USER_CUBE_HIER_VIEW_COLUMNS` describes the columns of the relational hierarchy views of the OLAP cube dimensions owned by the current user. Its columns (except for `OWNER`) are the same as those in `ALL_CUBE_HIER_VIEW_COLUMNS`.
