`USER_MVIEW_DETAIL_PARTITION` displays freshness information for all materialized views, with respect to a PCT detail partition, owned by the current user. Its columns are the same as those in ["ALL\_MVIEW\_DETAIL\_PARTITION"](statviews_1146.md).
