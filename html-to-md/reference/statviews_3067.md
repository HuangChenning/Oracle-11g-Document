`DBA_ARGUMENTS` lists the arguments of the functions and procedures that are available in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_ARGUMENTS`.

See Also:

- ["ALL\_ARGUMENTS"](statviews_1016.md)
- ["DBA\_PROCEDURES"](statviews_4186.md) for information about the functions and procedures that are available in the database
