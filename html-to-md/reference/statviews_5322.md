`USER_IND_SUBPARTITIONS` describes, for each index subpartition owned by the current user, the partition-level partitioning information, the storage parameters for the subpartition, and various partition statistics generated by the `DBMS_STATS` package. Its columns are the same as those in ["ALL\_IND\_SUBPARTITIONS"](statviews_1108.md).
