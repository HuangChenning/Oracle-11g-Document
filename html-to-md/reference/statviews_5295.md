`USER_EPG_DAD_AUTHORIZATION` describes the DADs that are authorized to use the user's privileges. Its columns (except for `USERNAME`) are the same as those in ["DBA\_EPG\_DAD\_AUTHORIZATION"](statviews_3169.md).
