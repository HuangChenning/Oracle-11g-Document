`USER_LOB_SUBPARTITIONS` displays partition-level attributes of the LOB data subpartitions owned by the current user. Its columns are the same as those in ["ALL\_LOB\_SUBPARTITIONS"](statviews_1131.md).

- `DBA_LOB_TEMPLATES` describes all LOB subpartition templates in the database.
