`USER_UPDATABLE_COLUMNS` describes columns in a join view that can be updated by the current user, subject to appropriate privileges. Its columns are the same as those in ["ALL\_UPDATABLE\_COLUMNS"](statviews_2129.md).

[Oracle Database Concepts](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/E11882_01/server.112/e40540/toc.htm) for information on updatable join views
