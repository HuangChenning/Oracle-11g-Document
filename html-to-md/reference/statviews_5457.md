`USER_SQLSET_STATEMENTS` displays information about the SQL statements, along with their statistics, that form the SQL tuning sets owned by the current user. Its columns (except for `SQLSET_OWNER`) are the same as those in ["ALL\_SQLSET\_STATEMENTS"](statviews_2074.md).
