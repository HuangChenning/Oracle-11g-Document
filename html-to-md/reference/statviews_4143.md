`DBA_MVIEW_DETAIL_SUBPARTITION` displays freshness information for all materialized views in the database, with respect to a PCT detail subpartition. Its columns are the same as those in ["ALL\_MVIEW\_DETAIL\_SUBPARTITION"](statviews_1148.md).
