`USER_IND_EXPRESSIONS` describes expressions of function-based indexes on tables owned by the current user. Its columns (except for `INDEX_OWNER` and `TABLE_OWNER`) are the same as those in ["ALL\_IND\_EXPRESSIONS"](statviews_1104.md).
