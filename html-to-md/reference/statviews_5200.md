`USER_ADVISOR_DIR_TASK_INST` displays information about all task directive instances owned by the current user. Its columns (except for `SEQ_ID` and `USERNAME`) are the same as those in ["DBA\_ADVISOR\_DIR\_TASK\_INST"](statviews_3015.md).
