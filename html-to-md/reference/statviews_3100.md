`DBA_CAPTURE_PREPARED_DATABASE` displays information about when the local database was prepared for instantiation. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_CAPTURE_PREPARED_DATABASE`.

- ["ALL\_CAPTURE\_PREPARED\_DATABASE"](statviews_1028.md)
