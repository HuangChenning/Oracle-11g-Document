`USER_PROCEDURES` lists all functions and procedures owned by the current user, along with their associated properties. Its columns (except `OWNER`) are the same as those in ["ALL\_PROCEDURES"](statviews_2013.md).

See Also:

["USER\_ARGUMENTS"](statviews_5228.md) for information about the arguments of the functions and procedures owned by the current user
