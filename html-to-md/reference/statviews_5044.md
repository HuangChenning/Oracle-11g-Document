`DBA_TAB_COLUMNS` describes the columns of all tables, views, and clusters in the database. Its columns are the same as those in ["ALL\_TAB\_COLUMNS"](statviews_2103.md). To gather statistics for this view, use the `DBMS_STATS` package.
