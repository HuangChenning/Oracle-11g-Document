`USER_MVIEW_DETAIL_SUBPARTITION` displays freshness information for all materialized views, with respect to a PCT detail subpartition, owned by the current user. Its columns are the same as those in ["ALL\_MVIEW\_DETAIL\_SUBPARTITION"](statviews_1148.md).
