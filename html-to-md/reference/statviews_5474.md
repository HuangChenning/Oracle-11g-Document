`USER_TAB_COL_STATISTICS` contains column statistics and histogram information extracted from ["USER\_TAB\_COLUMNS"](statviews_5476.md). Its columns are the same as those in ["ALL\_TAB\_COL\_STATISTICS"](statviews_2101.md).
