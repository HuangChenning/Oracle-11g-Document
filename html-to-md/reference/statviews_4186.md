`DBA_PROCEDURES` lists all functions and procedures that are available in the database, along with their associated properties. Its columns are the same as those in ["ALL\_PROCEDURES"](statviews_2013.md).

See Also:

["DBA\_ARGUMENTS"](statviews_3067.md) for information about the arguments of all of the functions and procedures that are available in the database
