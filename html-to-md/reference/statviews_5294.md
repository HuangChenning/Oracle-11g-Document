`USER_ENCRYPTED_COLUMNS` maintains encryption algorithm information for all encrypted columns in all tables in the user's schema. Its columns (except for `OWNER`) are the same as those in ["ALL\_ENCRYPTED\_COLUMNS"](statviews_1085.md).
