`DBA_TAB_PARTITIONS` displays partition-level partitioning information, partition storage parameters, and partition statistics generated by the `DBMS_STATS` package for all partitions in the database. Its columns are the same as those in ["ALL\_TAB\_PARTITIONS"](statviews_2108.md).
