`DBA_DIM_ATTRIBUTES` describes the relationships between dimension levels and functionally dependent columns in the database. The level columns and the dependent column must be in the same table. This view's columns are the same as those in ["ALL\_DIM\_ATTRIBUTES"](statviews_1071.md).
