`USER_IND_COLUMNS` describes the columns of the indexes owned by the current user and columns of indexes on tables owned by the current user. Its columns (except for `INDEX_OWNER` and `TABLE_OWNER`) are the same as those in ["ALL\_IND\_COLUMNS"](statviews_1103.md).
