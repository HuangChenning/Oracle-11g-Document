## 49 KFOD-00316 to KFOD-00403

KFOD-00316: Error acquiring ASM disk discovery string from Grid Plug and Play profile: call to \[string\] failed.

Cause: Call to the Grid Plug and Play service to request disk discovery string failed.

Action: Ensure that the Grid Plug and Play component of Cluster Ready Services is available. If Grid Plug and Play is operational and command still fails, contact Oracle Support Services.
